Placement

A real-proportioned model fit to a 1-cell footprint is routinely much taller than this game's own furniture ever renders — every def caps tall at 10-36px (chair:10, sofa:20, fridge:30, lamp:36 — see objects.js), and sprites.js's drawImage squashes the whole sprite into that shorter box rather than cropping it. This slider previews that exact squash so the critter comparison means something; it's never baked into the export — the PNG always keeps the model's true proportions, same as every other sprite already in the game, and the game applies its own def's tall at draw time.
Runs automatically on load. Re-run after changing footprint cells — it scales the model's own X/Z extent to just fill W×H and re-grounds it; nudge from there by hand.

Framing

Zoom scales the whole view around the piece's own ground anchor. Margin only pads the top/sides — the bottom edge always sits exactly on the anchor, with no slack, so the export needs no cropping.
